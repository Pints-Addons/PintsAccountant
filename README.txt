Pint's Accountant v1.0.0 - where your gold comes from and where it goes
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsAccountant
  Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Every copper you gain or spend is sorted by where it came from, by watching which window is
  open when your money changes (vendor, trainer, flight master, mailbox, auction house, bank,
  trade, loot, quest giver and so on).

  The Ledger shows each category for: this Session, Today, Week, Month, Year and All time,
  with totals and your net gain or loss. Today starts at the server's daily reset
  (8:00 am server time on most realms).
  - Income: vendor sales, looted gold, quest rewards, auction sales and refunds, mail, trades,
    guild bank withdrawals, other income.
  - Expenses: repairs, gear, consumables & reagents, recipes, mounts, other vendor purchases,
    buyback, class and profession training, flight paths, auction deposits and purchases,
    postage, mail and COD, trades, guild bank deposits, talent resets, bank slots, guild and
    tabard fees, barber, other expenses.
  - Gold per hour, session length and gold on hand.
  - Switch between characters, or see the whole account, from the menu at the top-left.
  - Optional on-screen gold display: character gold, account gold, session gain or loss.

HOW TO USE
  Click the minimap button (left-click: Ledger, right-click: options), or type /pa.

COMMANDS
  /pa              open or close the Ledger
  /pa options      options (on-screen display, what it shows, lock)
  /pa minimap      show or hide the minimap button
  /pa reset        reset the gold display and button positions (after /reload)
  /pa button       show or hide the loose Accountant button
  /pa forget Name-Realm   remove another character's records

NOTES
  Totals start from when the add-on is installed - it can't see gold earned before that.
  Money that arrives with no window open is counted as "Other income" / "Other expenses".
