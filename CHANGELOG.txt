Pint's Accountant - Changelog

v1.0.3
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v1.0.2
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- Forever ledgers saved under a first name alone (before this fix) are cleared once.

v1.0.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item and add-on functions where the old ones are gone.

v1.0.0
- First public release.
- Ledger: income and expenses by source for Session, Today (from the daily server reset), Week,
  Month, Year and All time,
  per character or the whole account, with net, gold per hour and gold on hand.
- On-screen gold display (character, account, session) and a minimap button.
- Gold paid to quest givers is counted under "Other expenses" (no separate Quest costs line).
