Pint's Accountant - Changelog

v1.0.0
- First public release.
- Ledger: income and expenses by source for Session, Today (from the daily server reset), Week,
  Month, Year and All time,
  per character or the whole account, with net, gold per hour and gold on hand.
- On-screen gold display (character, account, session) and a minimap button.
- Gold paid to quest givers is counted under "Other expenses" (no separate Quest costs line).
