Pint's Accountant - Changelog

v1.1.3
- Ledger: the "Dates" column only shows when "Show Dates column" (bottom of the ledger) is ticked - otherwise it's not
  there at all.
- Ledger: a Reset button in the Dates picker puts the column back to just today.

v1.1.2
- Ledger: categories with nothing in any column are left out, and show up as soon as something lands in them.
  A "Hide empty rows" box at the bottom of the ledger turns this on or off (so does /pa zeros).
- Ledger: a "Dates" column for any day or range of days you pick - click its heading for From / To (Shift: a week
  at a time), Single day, and quick picks (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 / 14 / 30). The days you pick are kept.

v1.1.1
- Currencies no longer take a tab each: the ledger shows one line per currency (Merchant's Favor, Honor Points...)
  at the bottom - how many you gained (or spent) this session, today, this week, month and year, and how many you have.
- Long character names (WoW Forever realms) stay inside the character button.

v1.1
- WoW Forever: the ledger has a tab for each currency next to Gold - Merchant's Favor, Honor Points and any other you
  earn. Each shows what came in (quest rewards, loot, honorable kills, other) and what went out (vendor purchases,
  other) for the session, today, week, month, year and all time, plus how much every character holds.
- Options: show your currencies on the on-screen gold display. The minimap button's tooltip lists them too.

v1.0.4
- WoW Forever: removes the leftover first-name-only characters ("Rain" next to "Rain Mon"), and they don't come back.

v1.0.3
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v1.0.2
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- Forever ledgers saved under a first name alone (before this fix) are cleared once.

v1.0.1
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item and add-on functions where the old ones are gone.

v1.0.0
- First public release.
- Ledger: income and expenses by source for Session, Today (from the daily server reset), Week,
  Month, Year and All time,
  per character or the whole account, with net, gold per hour and gold on hand.
- On-screen gold display (character, account, session) and a minimap button.
- Gold paid to quest givers is counted under "Other expenses" (no separate Quest costs line).
